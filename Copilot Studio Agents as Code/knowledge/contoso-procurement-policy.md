# Contoso Procurement Policy

**Effective 1 January 2026 · Version 4.2 · Owner: Procurement Operations**

This policy governs all purchases of goods and services made on behalf of Contoso by any employee, contractor, or business unit.

## 1. Approval thresholds

All values are in USD and refer to the **total contract value**, including options, renewals, and estimated usage over the full term — not the initial invoice.

| Band | Total contract value | Sourcing requirement | Approval required |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Under $5,000 | Direct purchase. No quotes required. | Line manager |
| B | $5,000 – $24,999 | Three written quotes | Department head |
| C | $25,000 – $99,999 | Formal RFQ through Procurement | Procurement Manager and Finance Director |
| D | $100,000 and above | Full RFP. Competitive bidding mandatory. | CFO and Legal |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to fall into a lower band is prohibited and is treated as a control violation.

## 2. Sole source and direct award

A direct award above Band A requires a written **Sole Source Justification** approved by the Procurement Director, regardless of value. Acceptable grounds are limited to:

- Only one vendor can supply the item or service, and this is demonstrable.
- Continuity with an existing system where switching cost demonstrably exceeds the benefit of competition.
- Genuine emergency affecting safety, security, or business continuity.

Vendor preference, prior relationship, convenience, and time pressure arising from poor planning are **not** acceptable grounds.

## 3. Required documentation

| Band | Required before purchase order is raised |
|---|---|
| A | Receipt or invoice |
| B | Three quotes, quote comparison summary, business justification |
| C | RFQ pack, scored evaluation matrix, recommendation memo |
| D | RFP pack, scored evaluation matrix, recommendation memo, Legal review, signed contract |

Documentation is retained for **seven years** from the end of the contract term.

## 4. Standard lead times

Plan against these. They are working days from complete requisition to purchase order.

| Band | Typical lead time |
|---|---|
| A | 2 days |
| B | 10 days |
| C | 25 days |
| D | 45 days, plus Legal review |

Requests that arrive with insufficient lead time are not expedited by default. Expedite requires Procurement Manager approval and a documented business reason.

## 5. Preferred vendors

Contoso maintains preferred vendor agreements in the following categories. Purchases from a preferred vendor within contracted terms may skip the quote requirement in Band B, but still require Department Head approval.

| Category | Preferred vendors |
|---|---|
| Office furniture | Steelcase, Herman Miller |
| IT hardware | Dell, Lenovo |
| Travel | Contoso Travel Desk (mandatory) |
| Professional services | Framework panel — contact Procurement |

Preferred vendor status is reviewed annually and does not remove the requirement for a purchase order.

## 6. Software and cloud services

Any software or cloud purchase, at any value including free tiers, additionally requires:

- **Security review** by Information Security before contract signature.
- **Data protection assessment** where personal data is processed.
- Confirmation that the agreement does not auto-renew without notice.

Departments may not accept click-through terms on behalf of Contoso.

## 7. Sustainability

Band C and D sourcing must include a sustainability criterion weighted at no less than **10%** of the total evaluation score. Vendors must supply an environmental statement for contracts of $100,000 and above.

## 8. Prohibited

- Committing to a vendor verbally or in writing before the required approval is in place.
- Purchasing from a vendor in which the requester or an immediate family member holds a financial interest, without a declared and approved conflict of interest.
- Accepting gifts or hospitality from a current or prospective vendor above $75 in value.
- Using personal funds with the expectation of reimbursement for purchases above $500.

## 9. Escalation and exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Procurement Director. Where this policy conflicts with a signed contract, the contract governs and Procurement must be notified.
